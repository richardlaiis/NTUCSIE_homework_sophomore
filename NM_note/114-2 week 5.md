## Cholesky
+ banded matrix: A是$n*n$方陣且對角線(含)左下$x$條斜線為元素，右上$y$條斜線為元素，其餘為0
+ principle submatrix: Let $S\subseteq\{1,2,\dots,n\}$, $S^C$ be its complement, the matrix $A_S$ is the original matrix with $i$-th row and column deleted $\forall i\in S^C$, called the principle submatrix.
	+ For the SPD, the principle submatrix is positive definite.
+ axpy: operation calculating $ax+y$, where $a$ is a scalar, and $x,y$ are vectors
