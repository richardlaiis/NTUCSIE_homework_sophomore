    \item Find the solution of \eqref{eq:1} by any method.

\section*{Problem 3 (15\%)}

In deriving the direction $p_k$ of conjugate gradient method, we use a lemma to know that we need to solve
\[
\min_{z} \| r_{k-1} - AP_{k-1}z \|.
\]
Then after some derivations, we have that
\begin{equation}
\| r_{k-1} - AP_{k-1}z \|^2 = \left(1 + \frac{\mu}{\alpha_{k-1}}\right)^2 \| r_{k-1} \|^2 + \left\| - \frac{\mu}{\alpha_{k-1}} r_{k-2} - AP_{k-2}w \right\|^2, \label{eq:2}
\end{equation}
where 
\[
z = \begin{bmatrix} w \\ \mu \end{bmatrix}
\]
Formally prove that if $w, \mu$ are the optimal solution of minimizing \eqref{eq:2}, then $-w / (\mu/\alpha_{k-1})$ must be the solution of
\[
\min_{z} \| r_{k-2} - AP_{k-2}z \|.
\]

\section*{Problem 4 (15\%)}

Given any linear system
\begin{equation}
Ax = b, \label{eq:3}
\end{equation}
with $A$ symmetric positive definite. Assume now we solve
\begin{equation}
P^T A P x = P^T b, \label{eq:4}
\end{equation}
instead, where $P$ is a permutation matrix.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item If we apply CG to solve both \eqref{eq:3} and \eqref{eq:4}, will the number of iterations be different? We assume exact operations without any numerical errors. We also assume that the stopping condition is when the exact solution of \eqref{eq:3} or \eqref{eq:4} has been found.
    \item How about vectors $r_k$, $p_k$ and scalars $\alpha_k$, $\beta_k$? If they are changed, what are the new values?
\end{enumerate}
